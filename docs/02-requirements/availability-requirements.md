# SlotWise — Availability and Time-Slot Generation Requirements

**Document:** Functional Requirements — Availability Management  
**Version:** 1.0 (Draft)  
**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Availability Calculation

**FR-AVAIL-001 — Automatic time-slot generation**

The system shall automatically generate available appointment time slots based on provider schedules, staff availability, service duration and existing appointments.

**FR-AVAIL-002 — Service selection**

The system shall use the selected provider service to determine the appointment duration and applicable booking rules.

**FR-AVAIL-003 — Staff eligibility**

The system shall consider only active, bookable staff members who are associated with the selected provider and eligible to perform the selected service.

**FR-AVAIL-004 — Working schedules**

The system shall generate candidate time slots using the recurring working schedules of eligible staff members.

**FR-AVAIL-005 — Availability exceptions**

The system shall exclude periods affected by staff leave, holidays, breaks and other configured availability exceptions.

**FR-AVAIL-006 — Existing appointments**

The system shall exclude time periods that overlap with existing appointments that occupy the staff member's schedule.

**FR-AVAIL-007 — Appointment duration**

Each generated time slot shall accommodate the full duration of the selected service and any applicable scheduling buffers.

**FR-AVAIL-008 — Available slot presentation**

The system shall present customers with available appointment start times for their selected provider, service and date.

**FR-AVAIL-009 — No available slots**

If no eligible staff member is available, the system shall inform the customer that no appointment slots are available for the selected date.

## 2. Appointment Booking

**FR-AVAIL-010 — Customer slot selection**

The system shall allow customers to select one of the generated available time slots.

**FR-AVAIL-011 — Slot revalidation**

The system shall revalidate the selected time slot before confirming the appointment.

**FR-AVAIL-012 — Automatic staff assignment**

The system shall automatically assign an eligible staff member who is available for the entire appointment period.

**FR-AVAIL-013 — Concurrent booking protection**

The system shall prevent concurrent booking requests from successfully reserving overlapping appointments for the same staff member.

**FR-AVAIL-014 — Booking failure handling**

If the selected slot becomes unavailable before confirmation, the system shall reject the booking attempt and allow the customer to retrieve updated availability.

## 3. Scheduling Configuration

**FR-AVAIL-015 — Booking interval**

The system shall support a configurable interval that determines the candidate appointment start times generated by the scheduling engine.

**FR-AVAIL-016 — Booking horizon**

The system shall support configurable booking limits, including how far in advance customers can book.

**FR-AVAIL-017 — Time-zone handling**

The system shall interpret provider schedules using the provider's configured time zone and consistently store appointment timestamps.

---

*The detailed slot-generation algorithm, default scheduling interval, buffer policies and booking horizon will be established during system design.*
