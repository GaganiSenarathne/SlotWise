# SlotWise — Availability Data Model

**Document:** Availability Data Model  
**Version:** 1.0 (Draft)  
**Status:** Proposed

## 1. Purpose

This document defines how SlotWise manages employee availability and dynamically generates appointment time slots.

The scheduling model supports both individual service providers and businesses with multiple employees.

Customers select a provider, service, date and one of the available time slots generated by SlotWise. The system automatically assigns an eligible employee when a booking is submitted.

## 2. Slot Generation Policy

SlotWise shall use a fixed, platform-wide appointment slot generation interval.

**Initial interval: 15 minutes**

The interval determines the frequency of candidate appointment start times.

Examples:

- 09:00
- 09:15
- 09:30
- 09:45
- 10:00

The interval is a platform-level configuration. Individual providers and employees cannot change it.

The platform interval does not determine the duration of an appointment. Appointment duration is determined by the selected service.

The interval shall be maintained as application configuration rather than as an employee-specific database attribute.

## 3. Employee Availability Management

Each bookable employee shall have an independently configurable availability schedule.

The system shall support:

- Recurring working days.
- Working start and end times.
- Break periods and unavailable periods.
- Additional working hours or special availability.
- Temporary leave and holidays.
- Changes to existing working schedules.

An authorized employee may manage their own availability, subject to the permissions configured by the provider.

Provider owners and administrators may also manage employee schedules according to their permissions.

For an individual provider, the individual professional shall be represented as a bookable employee.

## 4. Availability Data Entities

### 4.1 AvailabilityRule

Stores recurring working schedules for bookable employees.

Proposed attributes:

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| id | Unique schedule identifier |
| provider_staff_id | Associated employee |
| day_of_week | Day of the week |
| start_time | Working start time |
| end_time | Working end time |
| effective_from | Date the schedule becomes effective |
| effective_until | Optional date the schedule ends |
| is_active | Whether the schedule is active |

### 4.2 AvailabilityException

Stores changes to an employee's normal working schedule.

Examples include:

- Annual leave.
- Sick leave.
- Public holidays.
- Break periods.
- Temporary unavailable periods.
- Additional working hours.

Proposed attributes:

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| id | Unique exception identifier |
| provider_staff_id | Associated employee |
| start_at | Exception start timestamp |
| end_at | Exception end timestamp |
| exception_type | Type of availability exception |
| reason | Optional explanation |

The exception model shall distinguish between unavailable periods and additional availability.

### 4.3 StaffService

Defines the services an employee is eligible to perform.

The scheduling engine shall only consider employees associated with the selected service.

### 4.4 Appointment

Stores confirmed and pending appointment bookings, including the assigned employee and scheduled time.

Appointments that occupy an employee's schedule must be considered when calculating availability.

## 5. Available Time-Slot Generation

The availability calculation shall consider:

1. Selected provider.
2. Selected service.
3. Service duration.
4. Applicable service buffers.
5. Requested appointment date.
6. Eligible bookable employees.
7. Employee working schedules.
8. Availability exceptions.
9. Existing appointments.
10. Provider time zone.

The system shall generate candidate appointment start times using the fixed platform interval.

A candidate shall only be returned if at least one eligible employee can accommodate the complete appointment duration and applicable buffers.

The system shall avoid returning duplicate time slots when multiple employees are available at the same time.

## 6. Appointment Booking and Availability Revalidation

Generated availability is informational and does not guarantee that a slot will remain available.

When a customer selects a slot:

1. The system shall identify eligible employees.
2. The system shall revalidate availability.
3. The system shall automatically select an eligible employee.
4. The system shall reserve the employee's time consistently.
5. The system shall create the appointment with a pending approval status.

Pending approval appointments shall temporarily occupy the assigned employee's schedule to prevent conflicting bookings.

The booking operation shall use appropriate database transaction and concurrency-control mechanisms.

If reservation fails because another request has occupied the slot, the booking shall not be confirmed. The customer shall be able to retrieve updated availability.

## 7. Time-Zone Handling

Each provider shall have a configured time zone.

Recurring working schedules shall be interpreted using the provider's local time zone.

Appointment timestamps shall be stored consistently using PostgreSQL timestamp-with-time-zone support.

The system shall account for time-zone differences when generating availability and presenting appointment times to customers.

## 8. Scheduling Constraints

- An employee cannot be assigned to overlapping appointments.
- An employee must be eligible to perform the selected service.
- An appointment must fit within the employee's available working period.
- Unavailable exceptions must be excluded from candidate availability.
- Appointments awaiting approval must temporarily block their assigned employee's time.
- Cancelled and rejected appointments shall no longer block availability.
- The fixed platform slot interval shall not be configurable by individual employees.

## 9. Implementation Considerations

Available slots shall initially be generated dynamically rather than persisted as individual database records.

The database shall store working schedules, availability exceptions and appointments. The scheduling service shall calculate availability from these records.

The availability calculation and booking operation shall be implemented separately.

The booking operation shall revalidate availability and protect against concurrent reservations.

## 10. Appointment Approval and Cancellation

### 10.1 Approval Authorization

Appointments shall be approved or rejected by authorized provider-side users.

Supported approvers include:

- Individual providers.
- Business owners.
- Authorized business administrators.
- Employees with appointment approval permissions.

The system shall enforce provider-level authorization when processing approval or rejection requests.

### 10.2 Pending Approval

New appointment requests shall initially have the status `PENDING_APPROVAL`.

The maximum pending duration has not yet been finalized and shall be configurable through the appointment approval policy.

While awaiting approval, the assigned employee's time shall remain unavailable for other bookings.

An expiry policy shall be defined during detailed business rule analysis.

### 10.3 Customer Cancellation

Customers shall be allowed to cancel confirmed appointments.

The system shall record the cancellation action, including:

- User who cancelled the appointment.
- Cancellation timestamp.
- Cancellation reason.

Cancellation deadlines and possible fees shall be defined separately.

### 10.4 Employee and Provider Cancellation

Authorized employees and provider users shall be allowed to cancel appointments.

The system shall record the user responsible for cancellation and the reason.

### 10.5 Provider Rejection

When an authorized provider-side user rejects an appointment:

- The appointment status shall change to `REJECTED`.
- A rejection reason shall be recorded.
- The customer shall be informed of the rejection.
- The customer shall be offered the option to select another available time slot.

The original rejected appointment shall remain in the appointment history.

### 10.6 Rescheduling

SlotWise shall support rescheduling of existing appointments.

When a provider-side user proposes a new time for an existing confirmed appointment, the appointment shall enter `RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED` while awaiting customer action.

The proposed new time shall be validated against current availability.

When the customer accepts the proposed time, the system shall revalidate availability and process the rescheduling operation.

When a customer chooses an alternative slot following a rejected booking, SlotWise shall create a new booking linked to the original appointment.

Rescheduling operations shall preserve the history of previous appointment times and status changes.

## 11. Appointment Status Management

The system shall support the following appointment statuses:

- `PENDING_APPROVAL`
- `CONFIRMED`
- `REJECTED`
- `RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED`
- `IN_PROGRESS`
- `COMPLETED`
- `CANCELLED`
- `EXPIRED`
- `NO_SHOW`

Valid status transitions shall be enforced by the appointment management service.

Every status transition shall be recorded with the responsible user, timestamp and optional reason.

## 12. Availability Impact of Appointment Statuses

The following statuses shall occupy the assigned employee's schedule:

- `PENDING_APPROVAL`
- `CONFIRMED`
- `RESCHEDULE_REQUESTED` (the original appointment time remains occupied until the reschedule is accepted or otherwise resolved).

The following statuses shall not occupy the employee's schedule:

- `REJECTED`
- `CANCELLED`
- `EXPIRED`

Completed appointments remain historical records and shall not occupy future availability.

A no-show appointment shall also remain a historical record and shall not occupy future availability after its scheduled end time.
